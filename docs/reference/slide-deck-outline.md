# CSC110 Final Project — Slide Deck Outline

**8–10 slides** in any tool (Google Slides, PowerPoint, Canva). Slides + live demo take **about 5 minutes**, then about 3
minutes of Q&A. Export a PDF to `docs/slides.pdf` **and** upload it to Canvas. `/demo-prep` drafts each slide's content;
you make it look good and say it in your own words. Short bullets on the slide; the explanation comes from you.

| # | Slide | What goes on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | App name, one-line pitch, your name, a screenshot |
| 2 | **The problem & the user** | Who uses it and what they need (from your proposal) |
| 3 | **Demo roadmap** | The 3–4 things you're about to show live (then switch to the app) |
| 4 | **Architecture** | Your class diagram (simplified), with one sentence: "Model classes hold data and rules; the UI calls them." |
| 5 | **A rule I'm proud of** | One consistency/validation rule: the code snippet (≤ 12 lines) and how it works |
| 6 | **Save & reopen** | Your save-file format (a few example lines) and how loading rebuilds objects |
| 7 | **CSC110 concepts in my code** | Your ⭐ top 3 from the concept map: concept → where → why it matters |
| 8 | **How I steered Claude** | 2–3 real moments from your steering log: what Claude did, what you changed, why |
| 9 | **What I learned / what's next** | One lesson about programming, one about working with AI, one feature you'd add |
| 10 | *(optional)* **Testing** | Your JUnit tests by name, and a bug they caught |

**Tips:** include screenshots as a backup in case the app misbehaves. For code: big font, a small snippet, the key line highlighted.
